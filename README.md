# Xintong Zhou

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, pursuing an Extended Major in Artificial Intelligence. I expect to graduate in June 2028.

My interests center on **AI agents, preference learning, and efficient language models**. I enjoy connecting model training with interactive systems: how an agent chooses an action, how its dialogue reflects the world state, and how to evaluate whether its behavior makes sense.

## Selected work

### [Prison Demo — Small Language Models for NPC Agents](https://github.com/Zn-Cu1207/prison-demo)

A multi-NPC simulation that connects a learned action-value model with a separate dialogue model and an interactive 3D environment. My work covered preference-data preparation, LoRA training, model integration, the FastAPI simulation backend, and the Three.js demo.

**Focus:** preference learning · agent decision-making · action–dialogue consistency · local inference

### Foundation Models at the Edge — HKUST UROP

Undergraduate research with **Prof. Song Guo**, May 2025–January 2026. I explored streaming speech recognition and language-model intent parsing for smart-home requests, with structured output validation, clarification, and rule-based fallbacks. I also studied RWKV, Mamba, and hybrid language-model architectures and experimented with Mamba inference on a remote GPU.

**Focus:** streaming interaction · structured intent parsing · efficient model inference

### [AI Image Generation Backend](https://github.com/Zn-Cu1207/generating_photo)

A Python backend prototype for prompt submission, generation-task tracking, and image management, organized into API, service, schema, and database layers.

**Stack:** FastAPI · SQLModel · SQLite · Pydantic · Python
